## What was built

- **Migration `20261024210829_tickets_group_swaps`:** `support_tickets` (sequential number), `ticket_messages`, `ticket_status_changes`, `ticket_attachments`, `group_swap_requests`, `group_swap_decisions`; membership source `SWAP`. Hand-written triggers and checks listed above; RLS on every new table.
- **Tickets:** `/tickets` — for students, a category selector (complaint / problem, suggestion / question, group swap) with a form that follows it, their requests waiting for an answer, and their tickets filtered by category and status; for staff, the queue with search, filters and pagination. `/tickets/<id>` — description and files, conversation, reply with files (internal note for staff), outcome, status history, status form for staff, and the swap summary for swap tickets.
- **Group swaps:** `/group-swaps/<id>` — the proposed exchange, progress, outcome and reason, and the reader's step: accept / decline (other student), withdraw (requester), approve / reject with reason (faculty). `/group-swaps` — the faculty review queue (waiting for review by default; stale requests flagged). `/group-swaps/history` — the audit history.
- **Notifications:** ticket replied, ticket status changed; swap requested, accepted, under review, declined, approved, rejected, withdrawn, closed — each sent once per event (dedupe keys).
- **Dashboard:** a "Tickets and requests" card (open tickets, swaps waiting for the student or for the faculty, unread notifications).
- **Navigation:** Support tickets and Group-swap requests are live.

## Decisions taken (and why)

1. **A swap request is a ticket plus a request row.** The ticket gives the student one history (§13.3) and the request carries the workflow; the ticket's status follows the request (waiting for the other student → open; with the faculty → in progress; decided → closed) and cannot be set by hand (`TICKET_MANAGED_BY_SWAP`). Messages on a swap ticket are allowed.
2. **The counterpart must have an active account** — only a signed-in student can consent (§14.3). An unknown seat, a student not in the target group, one without an active account, or a typed name that does not match all return the same `SWAP_COUNTERPART_NOT_FOUND`, so the form cannot be used to probe who is in which group; requests are limited to 10 per student per hour.
3. **Name check (§14.1):** passes on the record's full name or at least two of its words in order (first and family name), with the same spelling normalization as account linking.
4. **One open request per student, as either side.** This is the simplest rule that makes "neither student already moved by a conflicting pending request" (§14.2) impossible to violate; enforced in the service under row locks and again by the database.
5. **Requests are bound to the groups they were made for.** Accept and approve recheck both current groups; any other group change closes open requests (`GROUP_CHANGED`) and both students are told. The faculty never approves an exchange the students did not agree to.
6. **The requester may withdraw** an open request (not in §14, but it lets a student who changed their mind free the other student without waiting for a decision); the other student is told.
7. **Decisions are stored with the decider's name and role** in `group_swap_decisions` (the audit history survives account renames) and in the audit log; student-facing responses never include them (§13.3).
8. **Ticket workflow:** Open → In progress (first staff reply) → Resolved / Closed; a student reply reopens a resolved ticket; closed tickets take no messages until staff reopen them; the note given with Resolved or Closed becomes the outcome shown to the student. Internal notes and their files are never sent to students.
9. **Attachments** reuse the private-storage pattern of visit files: content-sniffed type (PDF, JPEG, PNG, WebP), 4 MB per file, at most 3 per post (4 MB in total through the web relay) and 10 per ticket, signed links only.
10. **Rate limits:** 10 tickets and 60 messages per student per hour.

## Known limitations

- A swap is always between two students; there is no one-way transfer request (by design, §14) — staff can still move a student from the group page, which closes that student's open requests.
- The staff queue for tickets has no assignment to a particular staff member.
- Ticket attachments cannot be removed once posted (history is kept).
